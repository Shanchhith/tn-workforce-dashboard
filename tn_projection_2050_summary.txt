SENSITIVITY, age at registration (inferred, not observed)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Registration is assumed at age 24 (MBBS entry at 18 plus 5.5 years). Age is
    never observed in the register, only inferred from year of registration, and
    retirement timing is the output most sensitive to it.
    Age assumed      Active 2025   Active 2050  Retirements 2050  Cumulative retirements 2026-50
    22                   155,889       531,587             1,532                          25,764
    23                   153,624       524,705             1,658                          26,669
    24                   151,248       517,657             1,692                          27,600
    25                   150,548       516,644             1,745                          28,898
    26                   149,738       515,463             1,978                          30,337
    Central case is 24. A one-year change moves the 2050 stock by well under one
    per cent but shifts cumulative retirements materially, which is the point:
    the stock is robust to this assumption, the retirement profile is not.

    CAVEAT: this is an UNCONSTRAINED SUPPLY projection. It answers 'how many
    doctors will Tamil Nadu produce and retain on current trajectories', not
    'how many will the health system employ'. A density approaching OECD
    levels is the model telling us that current seat-expansion plans exceed
    any plausible absorption capacity, that is the finding, not a forecast
    of employment. The corrective in reality is higher out-migration, which
    the sensitivity block above prices.
